Move With Me

Gentle tai chi, guided by your camera

Your camera picture will appear here.

Press Start camera when you are ready.

Before you start

  • Clear a space about two steps wide around you.
  • Stand two to three metres back so the camera sees you from head to feet.
  • Face a window or lamp so your body is well lit.
  • Keep a sturdy chair nearby for balance if you need it.

Your privacy: the video is processed in this browser and is never uploaded or recorded.

Joint angles

Bend an arm or a knee to see the numbers change. These will be used to check each move.

Move gently and within a comfortable range. Stop if you feel pain, dizziness or shortness of breath. This prototype is not medical advice.
